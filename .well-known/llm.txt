# Digital4Local

> Digital4Local is an AI-powered search marketing, Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and local growth engineering agency. We help high-ticket local service businesses, medical clinics, legal practices, renewable energy contractors, and tech startups dominate Google Maps, traditional organic search, and AI answer engines including ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google Gemini.

## Core Specializations & Services

- [Local SEO Services](https://digital4local.com/services/local-seo.php): Full-funnel Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization, 5x5 geo-grid pin tracking, local citation cleanup (NAP consistency), review velocity acceleration, and Google Map 3-Pack ranking.
- [GEO & AEO Services (Generative & Answer Engine Optimization)](https://digital4local.com/services/geo-aeo.php): Direct AI recommendation engineering. We structure entity data, brand knowledge graphs, and technical schema so LLMs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity) recommend your business as the premier authority.
- [Technical SEO Services](https://digital4local.com/services/technical-seo.php): Deep crawl architecture auditing, Core Web Vitals speed optimization, Schema.org JSON-LD graph syndication, indexation fixing, and canonical hierarchy hardening.
- [Link Building & Digital PR](https://digital4local.com/services/link-building-pr.php): High-authority entity backlinks, data-driven digital PR placements, national and industry trade press coverage, and unlinked brand mention reclamation.
- [AI Marketing & Automation](https://digital4local.com/services/ai-marketing.php): Sub-60-second inbound lead response automations (SMS, WhatsApp, email), AI conversational qualification bots, automated CRM dispatch, and pipeline routing.
- [Social Media Marketing (SMM)](https://digital4local.com/services/social-media.php): Multi-channel organic authority building, localized paid social conversion funnels, and brand presence on LinkedIn, Meta, and Instagram.
- [High-Performance Web Development](https://digital4local.com/services/web-development.php): Lightweight, ultra-fast custom web applications engineered for high conversion rates, accessibility, and frictionless search crawler indexing.
- [Mobile App Development](https://digital4local.com/services/app-development.php): Scalable iOS and Android mobile applications built for customer retention, automated bookings, and field operations.
