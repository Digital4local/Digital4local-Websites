## Frequently asked questions

### What does a digital marketing agency do?

A digital marketing agency plans and runs your online marketing: search engine optimisation, paid ads on Google and social media, content, websites and reputation management. A good agency also sets up tracking, so you know how many leads and sales each channel produces and where to spend more or less.

### Which digital marketing channel is best for a small business in Bhopal?

For most local businesses, Google Business Profile and local SEO give the best long-term return because they capture people already searching nearby. Google Ads works when you need leads quickly. Instagram and Meta ads suit businesses that sell visually or run seasonal offers, such as salons, cafes and coaching institutes.

### Should I choose SEO or Google Ads?

Google Ads brings leads within days but stops when you stop paying. SEO takes 3 to 6 months to build but keeps producing enquiries without per-click costs. Many Bhopal businesses start with Google Ads for immediate leads while SEO builds, then reduce ad spend as organic leads grow.

### How do you measure digital marketing results?

We track calls, WhatsApp clicks, form enquiries and, where possible, sales from each channel using call tracking and Google Analytics 4. Every month you get a short report showing leads, cost per lead and what changed, so decisions are based on numbers rather than likes or impressions.

We ask for an initial three-month commitment, because SEO and ad optimisation need time to show real results. After that, plans continue month to month. [[VERIFY contract terms]]
