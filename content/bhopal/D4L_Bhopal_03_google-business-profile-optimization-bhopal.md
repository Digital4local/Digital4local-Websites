Frequently asked questions

Is Google Business Profile free?

Yes. Creating and managing a Google Business Profile is free from Google. You pay an agency like Digital4Local only for the time and expertise to optimise and manage it. Anyone asking you to pay Google for a profile or for a “verified badge” is not from Google.

How do I rank higher on Google Maps in Bhopal?

Choose the most accurate primary category, complete every profile field, keep your name, address and phone identical everywhere online, add real photos regularly, and earn a steady flow of genuine reviews with replies. Your distance from the searcher also matters, so focus first on the areas closest to you.

Why was my Google Business Profile suspended?

Common reasons include adding keywords to the business name, using a virtual or shared address, making many edits at once, or being reported by a competitor. The fix is to correct the issue, gather documents such as a GST certificate or utility bill, and submit a reinstatement request to Google.

How many reviews do I need to rank on Google Maps?

There is no fixed number. What matters is having more recent, genuine reviews than the businesses ranking near you, with replies from you. A clinic competing with rivals who have 300 reviews needs a steady monthly flow, not a one-time burst, which can look suspicious to Google.

How often should I post on Google Business Profile?

We recommend at least one post a week. Posts do not directly guarantee higher rankings, but an active profile with fresh photos, offers and updates converts more viewers into calls and signals to Google that the business is open and operating.
