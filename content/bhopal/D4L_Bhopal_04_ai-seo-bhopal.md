How AI tools decide which Bhopal businesses to mention

Nobody outside these companies knows the exact rules, and anyone claiming to is overselling. From our testing, the businesses that get named tend to share these traits:

Clear, consistent identity. The same name, address, phone and description across your website, Google Business Profile, JustDial, Practo, LinkedIn and other listings.

Third-party mentions. Being discussed on local news sites, review platforms, comparison lists and forums, not just on your own site.

Reviews with detail. Reviews that mention specific services and areas (“root canal at their Kolar clinic”) give AI tools concrete facts to repeat.

Pages that answer real questions. Direct, factual answers near the top of the page are easier to extract than marketing slogans.

Structured data. Schema markup tells machines exactly what you are, where you are and what you offer.

Frequently asked questions

What is GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)?

GEO is the practice of improving how often and how accurately AI tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity mention your business in their answers. It focuses on consistent business information, credible third-party mentions, structured data and clear, factual content that AI systems can confidently quote.

What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)?

AEO is optimising content to be selected as the direct answer to a question, in featured snippets, “People also ask” boxes, voice assistants and Google AI Overviews. It relies on question-led headings, short direct answers near the top of a page, and FAQ schema markup.

Is AI SEO different from normal SEO?

It builds on normal SEO rather than replacing it. AI tools still rely heavily on search indexes and trusted websites. AI SEO adds work on entity consistency, third-party mentions and answer-first content, so your business is not only found but also named and correctly described inside AI answers.

How do I check if ChatGPT recommends my business?

Ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity the questions your customers would ask, such as “best physiotherapist in Bhopal for sports injuries”, in English and in Hindi. Note who is named and what is said about you. Our free AI visibility check does this systematically across dozens of prompts.

How long does AI SEO take to show results?

Most businesses see early changes in Google AI Overviews and Perplexity within 2 to 3 months, because these pull from live web results. Changes in ChatGPT and Gemini answers can take 3 to 6 months or longer, depending on how quickly those tools refresh what they know.
